After years of designing, playtesting, and reprinting more card decks than I care to admit, I'm excited to share that Clown Migration Plan is now live on [Kickstarter](https://www.kickstarter.com/projects/jgarverick/clown-migration-plan-a-co-no-op-deckbuilder).

## What is Clown Migration Plan?

Anyone who has spent time in tech has at least one story about a project that went sideways, whether it was a reorg in the middle of a release, a sprint torched by scope creep, or leadership that seemed actively hostile to success. Clown Migration Plan puts you in charge of a cloud migration your company is counting on, and then throws all of that at you while you try to hit your deadline. It's a strategic deckbuilding and resource management game for one to four players, and a game runs 45 to 90 minutes for ages 14 and up.

Every card in the game is based on real people, events, and disasters from my 20-plus years working in tech. It's meant to be difficult, frustrating, and darkly funny, much like the real thing. You don't need an industry background to enjoy it, though; if you like Ascension, Terraforming Mars, or Illuminati: New World Order, you should feel right at home.

## How it plays

Each player manages a project made up of a Board, which is your leadership team; a Backlog of work you need to complete; and a Bench of cards you can play each turn. The center of the table holds the Event Stream, a constantly shifting row of cards you can acquire to build your deck or wreck your opponents. Every turn follows three phases:

1. **Sprint Planning:** Draw cards and resolve your Backlog.
2. **Sprinting:** Play cards, make your moves, and buy from the Event Stream.
3. **Sprint Review:** Make final adjustments before the next player's turn.

Of course, your leadership isn't always on your side. Some leaders boost your team, while others cut your resources or help your opponents, and two of your five leaders start face-down until the mid-game reveal. That's where the "co-no-op" comes from: you can play competitively or cooperatively, but either way, expect betrayal, unexpected alliances, and moments where everything goes perfectly wrong.

You win by clearing your entire Backlog before the final round or by maxing out your Influence meter. In competitive mode, the highest combined score of Backlog progress, Influence, and deck quality takes it.

## Where it came from

This game started life as a conference talk in 2019, which was my way of categorizing all the heroes and villains I'd run into over the years. As I organized the personas, their abilities, and the events they caused, I realized I was building something bigger than a presentation; it reminded me a lot of the Monster Manual from my D&D days. A few years of moderate procrastination and a global pandemic later, here we are.

## About the campaign

The campaign runs for 45 days, from February 16 through April 2, with a funding goal of $30,000 and an estimated delivery of October 2026. There are four reward tiers:

| Tier | Price | What's included |
| --- | --- | --- |
| No-Frills (cards only) | $35 | All of the cards, plus any unlocked digital stretch goal rewards |
| Standard (core game) | $55 | The complete base game: 117 cards, the Event Stream gameboard, four player boards, and all tokens |
| Team Lead (deluxe) | $90 | Everything in Standard, plus the Alpha expansion (players five and six) and the Beta expansion |
| Principal Architect (all-in) | $169 | Everything in Team Lead, plus premium neoprene playmats, Kickstarter-exclusive promo cards, a limited edition book with the backstory behind every card in the core game, and all physical stretch goal rewards |

Shipping isn't charged during the campaign. After it ends, backers get a pledge manager survey to confirm their address and pay the actual shipping cost for their region, which keeps the rates fair no matter where you live.

There are stretch goals as well, starting with Trait Tokens at $50,000 and neoprene player mats at $85,000. At $150,000, everyone gets the Dumpstee Award, a mini dumpster fire trophy to commemorate surviving the project, and at $175,000, the Gamma expansion adds Companies and Consequences to the game.
