Aloha! I'm excited to share that my latest book (co-authored with the incomparable [Omar McIver](https://www.amazon.com/stores/Omar-Dean-McIver/author/B0BW5C8SZP)) has hit the press. [Implementing Event-Driven Microservices Architecture in .NET 7](https://www.packtpub.com/en-us/product/implementing-event-driven-microservices-architecture-in-net-7-9781803230405) by Packt follows a ground-up implementation of an application using EDA to better distribute, handle, and process events for a high-throughput transportation platform. Covering everything from message brokers and domain modeling to observability and advanced scalability strategies, this book intends to give a practitioner the skills needed to design, understand, build, and deploy an application in Azure leveraging EDA design principles. Feel free to cruise over to the Packt site to check it out, or head to [Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0BMVT95QG) to read the reviews or get a copy for yourself!
